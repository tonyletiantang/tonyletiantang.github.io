title: "Learning Noise-Robust Stabilizer Structure via Bell Sampling"

category: articles
excerpt: 'Joint work with Thomas Steckmann. Bell sampling efficiently learns the stabilizer group of low-magic pure states, but real devices prepare noisy states that generically have no exact Pauli stabilizers. We show that the ideal stabilizer group remains encoded in the noisy state as the Pauli operators under which it is invariant, and give Bell-sampling algorithms that learn it for states of nullity at most $$t$$. A finder-verifier scheme extracts the structure from partially corrupted sample batches. For sufficiently weak depolarizing noise, both samples and runtime are $$\mathrm{poly}(n,t,1/\varepsilon)$$.'
